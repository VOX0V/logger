Export to your Excel logbook

Send your current Excel logbook: the app adds to it the flights of your logbook database that are not there yet, in the empty flight rows of each year sheet. Rows that are already filled, formulas, totals and formatting are never modified; calculations are still done by Excel when it opens. Your original file is not touched: you download an updated copy. The copy is deleted from the server after the download (after an hour at the latest) and the file you sent is not kept.
